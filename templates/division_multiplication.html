Smart shortcuts to multiply faster — impress your teacher! 😄

×2

Double it!

Multiplying by 2 is just adding the number to itself.

7 × 2 = 7 + 7 = 14
45 × 2 = 45 + 45 = 90
×4

Double, then double again

×4 is the same as ×2 twice. Just double it twice!

6 × 4 → 6×2 = 12 → 12×2 = 24
9 × 4 → 9×2 = 18 → 18×2 = 36
×5

Half, then ×10

Multiply by 10, then halve it. Answers always end in 0 or 5!

8 × 5 → 8×10 = 80 → 80÷2 = 40
13 × 5 → 13×10 = 130 → 130÷2 = 65
×9

Finger trick! Digits add to 9.

Hold up 10 fingers. For n × 9, fold down finger n. Fingers left = tens, fingers right = ones. Digits of every multiple of 9 add up to 9.

4 × 9 → fold finger 4 → 3 left, 6 right = 36 (3+6=9 ✓)
7 × 9 = 63 → 6+3 = 9 ✓
×10

Add a zero!

Just write a 0 at the end — shift every digit one place left.

37 × 10 = 370
8 × 10 = 80
×11

Repeat the digit (1–9), sandwich the sum (2-digit)

Single-digit × 11: write the digit twice. Two-digit × 11: write first digit, add both digits for middle, write last digit.

6 × 11 = 66  |  8 × 11 = 88
34 × 11 → 3 _ 4, middle = 3+4=7 → 374
×12

Break it: ×10 + ×2

Multiply by 10 and by 2 separately, then add.

7 × 12 = (7×10) + (7×2) = 70 + 14 = 84
9 × 12 = 90 + 18 = 108
n²
ends ×5

Squaring numbers ending in 5

Take the tens digit, multiply by (tens digit + 1), then put 25 on the end.

25² → 2×3 = 6 → 625
35² → 3×4 = 12 → 1225
